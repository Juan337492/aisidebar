## Install in 3 steps

**1. Set up the companion app** (paste this into Terminal):

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Juan337492/aisidebar/main/install.sh | bash
```

It installs the small helper app that connects the sidebar to your Claude and Google accounts, starts it automatically, and tells you if Claude Code or Antigravity is missing.

**2. Add the extension to your browser** (download it below, under **Assets**):

- **Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc:** download `ai-sidebar-chrome.zip` and unzip it. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode** (top right), click **Load unpacked**, and pick the unzipped folder.
- **Firefox:** if there's an `ai-sidebar-firefox.xpi` file below, download it, open `about:addons`, click the ⚙️ menu, choose **Install Add-on From File…** and pick it. Otherwise download `ai-sidebar-firefox.zip`, open `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox`, click **Load Temporary Add-on…** and pick the zip. That way lasts until you restart Firefox.

**3. Connect and sign in.** Click the AI Sidebar toolbar icon (or press **Alt+Shift+A**), click **Connect**, click **Allow** on your Mac, then **Sign in with Claude** or **Sign in with Google**.

Full guide: https://github.com/Juan337492/aisidebar#install
